🌿 Science – What I Know About LEAVES
🌱 Leaves are called the "Kitchen of the Plant"
🔬
Stomata
Tiny holes on leaves that let gases in and out — like a little door!
🍃
Leaf Blade (Lamina)
The flat, wide part of the leaf — the big green part you can see!
🌿
Petiole
The stalk that joins the leaf to the stem — like a handle!
☀️
Photosynthesis
How green leaves make food using sunlight, air and water — like cooking with sunlight!
💚
Chlorophyll
The green colour inside leaves that helps catch sunlight — like a solar panel!
🥗
Edible Leaves
We can eat: Cabbage 🥬, Lettuce 🥗, Spinach 🌿
🏔️ Geography – Landforms I Know
🏔️

Mountains

The highest places on Earth! They have steep sides and a pointed top.

🏝️

Island

Land surrounded by water on all 4 sides! Like Sir Bani Yas Island in UAE.

🏞️

Valley

Low areas between mountains. Rivers often run through valleys!

🏜️

Desert

A very dry place! Can be hot with sand like the Rub' al Khali in UAE.

🌾

Plains

Large flat areas of land, good for farming and building cities!
